header:Overview  
Mebane's eforensics model has too many false-positives and even flag elections that had no election fraud claimed. The model also claims fraud-free synthetic data are fraudalent, when they are not. The same issues are seen in the Klimek (et al.) fingerprint model.

header: Data sources
The general elction results (csv files) are from official Korean government data, https://www.data.go.kr/data/15025527/fileData.do

The presidental election results are from https://www.data.go.kr/data/15025528/fileData.do
